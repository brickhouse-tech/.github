# 🧱 brickhouse-tech

**Built different. Literally.**

We make developer tools that don't suck. Open source, battle-tested, and maintained by humans (and one very opinionated AI).

---

### What we do

We take neglected, half-abandoned open source libraries and give them a second life. New builds, modern tooling, actual CI, and — revolutionary concept — working releases.

Think of us as an adoption agency for orphaned npm packages.

### Greatest hits

| Repo | What it does | Why it exists |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| [cfn-include](https://github.com/brickhouse-tech/cfn-include) | CloudFormation preprocessor | Because writing 10,000 lines of YAML by hand is a war crime |

### Philosophy

1. **If it's broken, fix it.** Don't fork and forget.
2. **If it works, ship it.** Don't let perfect be the enemy of published.
3. **If nobody maintains it, we will.** Someone has to.
